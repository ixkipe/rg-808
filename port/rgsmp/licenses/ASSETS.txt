RG-SMP sample packs
===================
The port ships user-provided WAV samples under assets/wav/:

  808/    TR-808 style one-shots
  707/    TR-707 style one-shots
  909/    TR-909 style one-shots
  DMX/    Oberheim DMX style one-shots
  E-MU/   E-mu Drumulator style one-shots
  LM1/    LinnDrum LM-1 style one-shots

The sample browser can also use any additional subdirectory placed under
assets/wav/ at runtime. Samples are loaded on demand, not converted into
track-specific family names.

Redistribution terms of these packs are not established in this repository.
The packs were supplied to the project by its owner. If this port is published
publicly, confirm the licence of every included sample first.
